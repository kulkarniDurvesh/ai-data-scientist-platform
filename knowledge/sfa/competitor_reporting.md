# GUIDE-MKT-05: Competitor activity reporting

*Synthetic document for testing. Fictional company "Arvana Pharma". Version 1.0, effective 1 March 2025.*

## What to report

- During a visit, if a doctor mentions or shows a competitor product, the MR records the competitor and product in the visit's *Competitor discussed* field.
- Also report competitor price changes, new launches and promotional events heard about in the territory.

## How the information is used

- Brand teams review competitor discussions monthly by territory and specialty.
- A territory where competitor discussions rise for two consecutive months is flagged for a brand review.

## Rules

- Report facts only: what was said or seen, by whom (doctor or pharmacist), and when.
- Do not collect competitor materials by improper means, and do not make claims about competitor products to doctors.
