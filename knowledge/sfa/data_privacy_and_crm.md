# POL-IT-07: Data privacy and SFA data quality

*Synthetic document for testing. Fictional company "Arvana Pharma". Version 1.1, effective 1 January 2025.*

## Doctor consent

- Doctors' personal contact details (mobile, personal email) are stored only with written or digital consent recorded in the SFA app.
- A doctor can withdraw consent at any time; the MR updates the record within 2 working days and stops using the personal contact details.

## Access

- MRs see only the doctors of their own territory. Area managers see their area; regional managers see their region.
- Exports of doctor lists to personal devices or personal email are not allowed.

## Data quality rules

- Visit records are entered on the same day; late entries over 3 days need a manager's reason.
- Duplicate doctor records are merged by the data team every month; MRs report suspected duplicates through the app.
- Follow-up dates must be on or after the visit date. Days since last visit can never be negative; negative values indicate a data error to be reported to the data team.

## Retention

Visit and order records are kept for 7 years. Inactive doctors' personal contact details are deleted after 2 years without a visit.
