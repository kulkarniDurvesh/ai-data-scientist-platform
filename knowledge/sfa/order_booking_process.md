# SOP-SAL-03: Order booking and follow-up

*Synthetic document for testing. Fictional company "Arvana Pharma". Version 1.4, effective 1 July 2025.*

## When an order is placed

- When a doctor agrees to prescribe or stock a product, the MR records the visit outcome as **Order Placed** and enters the products discussed, the estimated number of units and the preferred pharmacy.
- Estimated revenue is calculated by the app as units × list unit price; MRs do not enter revenue by hand.

## Pharmacy fulfilment

- The order is sent to the doctor's preferred pharmacy (or the nearest linked pharmacy) the same day.
- The pharmacy confirms stock within 2 working days; unfulfilled lines are flagged to the MR.

## Follow-up

- Every order gets a follow-up visit or call within **21 days** to confirm that the product was prescribed and stocked.
- If the doctor reports a problem (side effects, availability), the MR records it and, for any suspected side effect, informs pharmacovigilance within 24 hours (see the product sheet).

## Reporting

- Order conversion rate = visits with outcome Order Placed / completed visits. Target: at least 8% per month per territory.
- Average order value is reviewed monthly by the area manager.
