# POL-CMP-04: Sample distribution policy

*Synthetic document for testing. Fictional company "Arvana Pharma". Version 2.0, effective 1 January 2025.*

## Scope

Applies to all free product samples handed to doctors by Medical Representatives.

## Limits

- A doctor may receive at most **4 sample packs of the same product per month**.
- Samples are given only to registered doctors whose specialty matches the product's therapy area (for example, cardiology products to cardiologists and general physicians).
- Samples of a product are not given in the first 30 days after its launch unless the brand team approves.

## Recording

- Every sample handed over is recorded in the SFA app during the visit, with product, quantity and batch number.
- The doctor acknowledges receipt digitally in the app. Paper acknowledgements are not accepted from 2025.

## Storage

- MRs keep samples in the company-issued cool bag; products marked "store below 25 °C" must not be left in vehicles.
- Expired samples are returned to the regional depot within 7 days of expiry; they are never handed out or thrown away.

## Audits

The compliance team audits a random 5% of sample records each quarter. Differences above 2% between stock and records are escalated to the regional manager.

## Prohibited

- Selling samples or exchanging them for any benefit.
- Giving samples to pharmacies, hospitals' stores or patients directly.
