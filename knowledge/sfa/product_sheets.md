# Product sheets (field use)

*Synthetic document for testing. Fictional products of the fictional company "Arvana Pharma". Not medical information: names, indications and doses are invented.*

## Product A — Cardivex 10 mg

- **Therapy area:** Cardiology.
- **Positioning:** once-daily tablet for blood-pressure control in adults.
- **Key messages:** one tablet a day; can be combined with lifestyle changes; well tolerated in older adults.
- **Not for:** pregnant women; patients with severe kidney impairment.
- **Pack:** strip of 10 tablets; list price INR 120 per strip. Store below 25 °C.

## Product B — Glucora 500 mg

- **Therapy area:** Diabetology.
- **Positioning:** twice-daily tablet supporting blood-sugar control in type 2 diabetes, with diet and exercise.
- **Key messages:** take with meals; regular monitoring of blood sugar recommended.
- **Not for:** patients with severe liver disease.
- **Pack:** strip of 15 tablets; list price INR 95 per strip.

## Product C — Respiflo inhaler

- **Therapy area:** Pulmonology.
- **Positioning:** maintenance inhaler for adults with chronic breathing conditions.
- **Key messages:** use every day even without symptoms; check inhaler technique at each visit.
- **Pack:** 120 doses; list price INR 640.

## Product D — Dermalin cream

- **Therapy area:** Dermatology.
- **Positioning:** topical cream for mild to moderate skin inflammation.
- **Key messages:** thin layer twice a day for up to 2 weeks.
- **Pack:** 20 g tube; list price INR 180.

## Safety reporting (all products)

Any suspected side effect reported by a doctor or patient must be passed to the pharmacovigilance team within **24 hours** using the "Report safety event" form in the SFA app. MRs do not give medical advice; they refer clinical questions to the medical information desk.
