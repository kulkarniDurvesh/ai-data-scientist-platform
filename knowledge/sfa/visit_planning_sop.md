# SOP-FF-01: Doctor visit planning

*Synthetic document for testing. Fictional company "Arvana Pharma". Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2025.*

## Purpose

This SOP defines how Medical Representatives (MRs) plan doctor visits each month so that high-value doctors are seen regularly, visits are spread evenly across the month, and every visit is recorded in the SFA app on the same day.

## Doctor classification

Doctors are classified once per quarter by the area manager using potential and prescribing history:

| Class | Who | Visits per month |
|---|---|---|
| A | Top 20% of doctors by potential in the territory | 3 |
| B | Next 30% by potential | 2 |
| C | Remaining doctors | 1 |

A newly added doctor starts as class C until the next quarterly review.

## Monthly plan

- The MR submits the next month's visit plan in the SFA app by the 25th of the current month.
- The area manager approves or returns the plan within two working days.
- An MR plans at most **12 visits per working day** and at least **8**, except on training days.
- Visits to the same doctor must be at least **7 days apart**.
- MRs visit only doctors in their own territory. Cross-territory visits need written approval from both area managers.

## Daily execution

- Check in at the doctor's clinic using the SFA app; the check-in records time and location.
- Record the outcome before the end of the day: *Order Placed*, *Interested*, *Not Interested* or *Follow-up Required*.
- A planned visit that does not happen is marked *Cancelled* with a reason; it is rescheduled within the same month when possible.

## Follow-ups

A visit with outcome *Follow-up Required* must have a follow-up date within **14 days**. Overdue follow-ups appear on the MR's home screen and in the manager's weekly review.

## Coverage targets

- Class A coverage: every class A doctor visited at least 3 times in the month (target 95% of class A doctors).
- Overall coverage: at least 85% of active doctors in the territory visited in the month.
- Visit completion rate (completed / planned) of at least 90%.
