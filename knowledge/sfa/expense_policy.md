# POL-FIN-02: Field expense policy

*Synthetic document for testing. Fictional company "Arvana Pharma". Version 4.1, effective 1 April 2025.*

## Daily allowance

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Local travel (own vehicle) | INR 9 per km, up to 120 km per day |
| Meals on field days | INR 450 per day |
| Overnight stay outside headquarters | up to INR 3,000 per night, with bill |
| Mobile and data | INR 1,000 per month, fixed |

## Claims

- Expenses are claimed in the SFA app weekly, by Monday for the previous week.
- Travel claims must match the day's visits: the distance claimed is checked against the check-in locations of that day.
- Claims older than 30 days are not reimbursed.

## Doctor-related spending

- Gifts to doctors are not allowed.
- Meals with doctors only as part of an approved scientific meeting, up to INR 1,500 per doctor.
- Sponsorship of conferences needs approval from the medical affairs head.

## Approval

Claims up to INR 10,000 per week are approved by the area manager; above that, also by the regional manager.
